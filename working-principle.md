# Working Principle

## Overview

The Electricity Billing System works by collecting customer details and electricity meter readings through a Java AWT graphical user interface. The entered values are processed to calculate electricity consumption, chargeable units, slab-based billing amount, and next-month prediction.

## Working Flow

```text
Enter Customer ID
        ↓
Enter Customer Name
        ↓
Enter Address
        ↓
Enter Previous Reading
        ↓
Enter Current Reading
        ↓
Click Generate Bill
        ↓
Calculate Chargeable Units
        ↓
Apply Free Units
        ↓
Apply Slab-Based Rates
        ↓
Calculate Total Bill
        ↓
Predict Next Month Usage
        ↓
Calculate Predicted Bill
        ↓
Display Bill
        ↓
Click Print Bill
        ↓
Print Generated Bill
